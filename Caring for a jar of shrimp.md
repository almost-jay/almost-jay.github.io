## Q&A

##### Can I just put water straight from the tap in?
No. Tap water contains chlorine, which is harmful to the microbiome.

##### A stem plant keeps floating around
Leave it, or replant it, it's your choice. Many plants will grow just fine floating around the water.

##### A plant is growing out of the water, is this normal?
